# RBF-SVM on the descriptor in the registered scenarios (2026-09-15)

Extension registered in `../../PROTOCOL.md` ("RBF-SVM on the descriptor in the registered scenarios") before running. Method `gabor_svm` (`comparison/gabor_svm.py`): the 22-dimensional Gabor + CIELAB descriptor of PolyGabor, the same cap of 350 vectors per class and random draw, standardization on the retained vectors, and `SVC` with RBF kernel, C = 1 and gamma = 'scale', without any use of validation data. Runs: `runs/2026-09-15_svm` (driver `comparison/svm_campaign.py`); tables: `summary.md` and the CSV files, generated by `comparison/svm_report.py`. The PolyGabor and CNN rows come from the registered campaign `runs/2026-09-12`, and the CNN training cost from the CPU-only runs of `runs/2026-09-14_cpu`. `FROZEN.sha256` records the checksums of the code, tables, predictions, models and benchmarks before any post-hoc analysis.

All 38 runs completed. The registered consistency check passed: the test predictions of the 16 full and scarce-label runs were identical to those of `svm_default` in `results/2026-09-15_descriptor` (`runs/2026-09-15_svm/consistency.json`). The campaign ran alone on the machine, CPU only, in 1,400 s.

## Scarce labels and full training

The test macro-F1 of `gabor_svm` was 0.459, 0.432, 0.573, 0.689, 0.738, 0.773, 0.813 and 0.878 with 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100 images per class and the full training set (means of seeds 42 and 43). It was higher than PolyGabor with and without augmentation at every budget. Among the models without external pretraining, it was higher than YOLO11n-cls from scratch at every budget and than ResNet-18 from scratch from 1 to 50 images per class; ResNet-18 from scratch was higher with 100 images (0.868) and with the full set (0.908). It was higher than the ImageNet-initialized ResNet-18 from 1 to 20 images per class and lower from 50 on (0.897 at 50). The ImageNet-initialized YOLO11n-cls was higher at every budget (0.539 to 0.951). The G-mean of `gabor_svm` was above zero at every budget in both seeds (minimum 0.253, with 2 images per class), which among all methods was otherwise the case only for the ImageNet-initialized YOLO11n-cls.

## Label noise

With 20% of the training labels corrupted (seed 42), the macro-F1 of `gabor_svm` changed from 0.876 to 0.874 ($-0.002$). The CNNs lost between 0.006 and 0.082, and the PolyGabor gain of +0.045 did not occur with the SVM.

## Class imbalance

With tumor, stroma, complex stroma and mucosa reduced to 10% (seed 42), `gabor_svm` reached a macro-F1 of 0.647 and a G-mean of 0.536, against 0.408 and 0.000 for PolyGabor with the registered rule, and 0.712–0.905 and 0.000–0.899 for the CNNs. The reduced classes were still poorly recognized, especially tumor (recall 0.09) and stroma (0.28), while complex stroma and mucosa reached 0.56 and 0.52. The SVM used no class weights, as registered.

## Image perturbations

Compared with PolyGabor (means of two seeds), `gabor_svm` was better under brightness (0.673 vs. 0.353), color shift (0.627 vs. 0.328), JPEG (0.843 vs. 0.717), rotation (0.802 vs. 0.634) and occlusion (0.401 vs. 0.317), and worse under blur (0.401 vs. 0.443), noise (0.366 vs. 0.437) and low resolution (0.424 vs. 0.429). Under color shift it was above both ResNet-18 configurations and YOLO11n-cls from scratch (0.268–0.600) and below the ImageNet-initialized YOLO11n-cls (0.925); under JPEG it was similar to the scratch CNNs (0.824–0.839). Under blur, noise, low resolution and occlusion it remained far below all CNNs (0.596–0.942).

## Leave-one-source-out

The pooled macro-F1 of `gabor_svm` was 0.715 [0.708–0.723], against 0.554 for PolyGabor, 0.700 for ResNet-18 from scratch, 0.753 for YOLO11n-cls from scratch and 0.789–0.799 for the ImageNet-initialized CNNs; its drop relative to the random split was 0.162 (PolyGabor 0.239, CNNs 0.109–0.208). Most of the gain over PolyGabor came from the background class, whose recall rose from 0.05 to 0.96: without the true background patches, the macro-F1 was 0.683 against 0.664 for PolyGabor, and it was the lowest among the other methods (0.733–0.789). Per class, the SVM recognized complex stroma (0.70 vs. 0.45) and mucosa (0.84 vs. 0.44) much better than PolyGabor across slides, but debris (0.42 vs. 0.72), stroma (0.61 vs. 0.69), tumor (0.72 vs. 0.81) and immune cells (0.56 vs. 0.62) worse, so the advantage of PolyGabor for debris and stroma across slides reported in the paper does not hold for the SVM.

In the paired bootstrap over the ten slide sources, the difference to PolyGabor was +0.163 (seed 42) and +0.160 (seed 43), with intervals that included zero ($-0.015$ to 0.221 and $-0.021$ to 0.221). The SVM was worse than both ImageNet-initialized CNNs in both seeds, with intervals that excluded zero; worse than YOLO11n-cls from scratch in seed 43 (interval excluding zero) and not distinguishable in seed 42; and not distinguishable from ResNet-18 from scratch in either seed.

## Cost

Seed 42, CPU. Training, including feature extraction, took 0.51 s with 10 images per class and 22.1 s with the full training set (PolyGabor: 0.57 s and 21.2 s; CNNs on the CPU: 20–44 s and 231–2,437 s), with a peak RSS of the training and evaluation process of 407 MB and 1,013 MB (PolyGabor: 392 MB and 1,240 MB; CNNs: 1.3–2.2 GB and 2.2–3.5 GB). The saved model occupied 7.3 kB with 2 images per class and 0.35 MB with the full set (PolyGabor: 4.8 kB and 0.65 MB), because the SVM stores its support vectors. In the single-thread benchmark, the median latency was 5.3 ms and the peak RSS 254 MB, against 19.0 ms and 280 MB for PolyGabor, 20.2–20.7 ms and 939–946 MB for ResNet-18 and 3.3–3.6 ms and 825–853 MB for YOLO11n-cls; the int8 LiteRT YOLO11n-cls of the quantization extension ran in 0.8 ms with 109 MB.

## Limits

Two seeds, and one seed for label noise and imbalance. The SVM hyperparameters are library defaults, not tuned for this data. All results come from the same ten slides of one institution.
